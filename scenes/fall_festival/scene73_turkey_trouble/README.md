## Synopsis

The third scene of the **Fall Festival** series. At the festival the kids won a huge frozen turkey; the professor promised to cook it by science, the doctor promised the healthiest stuffing. Now it is Thanksgiving morning, the guests come at two, and the brothers, in aprons, heave the prize onto the island: THUD. The doctor's watch says 10:58. *"Relax. Thermodynamics will handle this."*

**Frozen solid.** A knock: CLONK. The stethoscope: *"No pulse."* One hair dryer, two hair dryers, a blown fuse; a hot bath in the sink with a rubber duck, and the bath freezes over. The wrap comes off, and the professor rules that the outside is thawed, which, by his calculation, is enough.

**The healthy stuffing.** Bright green kale, broccoli and quinoa (*"Zero fat. Zero sugar." "Zero fun?" "…Zero fat."*). The doctor stuffs the bird up to his elbow and comes out without his wristwatch. He looks at his wrist, at the bird, at us, shrugs and whistles.

**Seasoning by science.** Half a gram of pepper, measured with lab glassware, until the lid falls off and the whole jar goes in. Ah… ah… AH-CHOO! The sneeze hits the flour bag and the kitchen goes white. *"Bless you." "…Thank you."*

**The escape.** The slippery bird squirts out of their hands, CLANGs off the pendant lamp, BONKs off the fridge and lands on the professor's head. He stumbles about blind until the doctor pulls it off: POP, SPLAT. *"Five-second rule!" "That is not science." "It is medicine."*

**The oven calculation.** A floating chalkboard: 4 h × 350° = 1 h × 1400°! *"Science!"* The dial is cranked past MAX. They sit down to chess; the professor dozes, the oven glows, smoke rolls along the ceiling. *"Do you smell… toast?"*

**Disaster.** DING, BEEP BEEP. The door opens on a WHOOMPH of smoke: a shrunken lump of charcoal. A short comic hold on two sooty faces, blink, blink, then two idea bulbs.

**The cover-up.** Towels fan the smoke out of the window. The professor paints the black bird with gravy; a drumstick SNAPs off. The doctor goes into surgeon mode (*"Scalpel."*, and gets parsley: *"…Parsley."*), pins the drumstick on with toothpicks and a band-aid, and they bury the rest in parsley, cranberries, orange slices and paper frills. TA-DA: perfect, from the front.

**Button.** DING-DONG: the guests! *"Nobody will know." "In my medical opinion… nobody will know."* The bandaged drumstick drops off with a plonk; the doctor clicks it back; both grin at us. Freeze, TO BE CONTINUED…, iris on the turkey.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Timing:** `T` in `scene.js` holds the start of every line and action beat. The lines (text, speed, hold) are in `asset.js`; if you move a line, move its entry in `T` too, and the music `cues` that hit on it.
- **Blocking:** `at(n, t)` says where each brother is. Behind the island they stand at Z 985 on hidden step stools (`ISLY` = 36 world units up), so the island hides them below the waist and their hands reach its top; at the sink, the oven and in front of the island they stand on the floor; while waiting they sit low (`SPOT.sit`). The professor's blind stumble and the doctor's chase follow the `STUMBLE` / `SAMI_CHASE` keyframes.
- **Hands on the island:** `W2L(P, a, worldPoint)` turns a world point into the rig's local hand target, so hands touch the turkey in every camera. After `front()`, the arms of the brothers behind the island are redrawn over its top (clipped above its front edge) by `TT3.dress(..., { _armsOnly: true })`; `_under: true` in a pose skips that (the doctor's arm inside the bird).
- **Poses:** `pose(n, t, P, a)` is one function for both brothers, organised by beat. Underscore keys put props in their hands (`_bowl`, `_spoon`, `_jar`, `_brush`, `_pot`, `_parsley`, `_drum`, `_towel`, `_mittL/_mittR`, `_hold`, `_panHeld`) and drive `TT3.dress` (`_flour`, `_soot`, `_watch`).
- **The turkey:** `bird(t)` gives its `TKY` state (frozen with wrap → frozen without → burnt → fixed, with `drumstickOff` 160.6–161.6); `island()` (the set's `onIsland` hook) draws it on the island, in its pan or on its platter; `inOven()` draws it on the rack; `overWorld()` draws it in hands, in flight (`flight(t)`), on the professor's head and on the floor. The cover-up stages are `coverUp()`: `TT3.withoutDrumstick` clips the right drumstick away, `TT3.glaze` paints the wipe, then the re-attached drumstick, toothpicks, band-aid, garnish and frills; at TA-DA it swaps to `TKY` `'fixed'` under a sparkle flash.
- **The kitchen:** `kitchen(t)` returns the set options: morning light, `mess` (rises at the sneeze, the floor SPLAT, the reveal and the cover-up), the ceiling `smoke` haze, sink `steam`, and `oven` (`on`, `glow`, `door`, the plume `smoke`, `dial` cranked to 1.32 = past MAX). `TT3.clock` redraws the wall clock (it races from 10:58 to 2:00 at the doorbell, `hour(t)`), `TT3.display` makes the backguard read 1400°.
- **Props** (`props.js`, namespace `TT3`): aprons drawn over the rigs by `TT3.dress` (which also redraws the arms over the apron with the rigs' own IK, `TT3.arms`), flour/soot filters, the watch, hair dryers, duck, kettle, ice, spoon, glassware, pepper jar, flour bag, clouds and puffs, brush, gravy pot, band-aid, toothpicks, parsley, drumstick, frills, garnish, towels, oven mitts, chessboard, title card and TO BE CONTINUED card.
