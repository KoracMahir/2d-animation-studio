## Synopsis

Sarah lands in Vale and walks into a supermarket still towing a Glen suitcase. Milk, bread, apples, and cheese are tagged in Vale Marks, and the big numbers look expensive. She covers a counter with sticky notes and a calculator, guesses the cart is about 40 Crowns, and pays 48. Then she opens Howmch, points the camera at the cheese tag, and the phone shows 200 Marks as 20.00 Crowns immediately. She puts the cheese back and keeps the milk: 90 Marks is 9 Crowns.
