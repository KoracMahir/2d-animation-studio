## Synopsis

The second scene of the **Fall Festival** series, straight after part 1: same afternoon, same booths. Alma still has her glow-in-the-dark sword, Jenna her giraffe, Housam his plush dragon and Danny his robot, and the professor still has the doctor's toss ring on his head (*"Nice halo, Professor." "It's for luck."*). The brothers vow: *"This time... we win!"*

**Bean bag toss.** The professor winds up, his lucky ring flies off and rolls away, and his bag sails over the board and knocks the top pumpkin off a stack. BONK! *"The wind shifted. Basic fluid dynamics."* Housam points out that the leaves are falling straight down. The doctor's bag goes high and lands in a student's popcorn. POOF! The student shrugs and keeps eating. *"Hand-eye coordination naturally declines after forty. It's in the literature."* Housam drops his bag on his foot, juggles it foot, knee, foot, and volleys it into the hole. Alma twirls, turns her back, closes her eyes and tosses hers over her shoulder. SWISH! WINNER! *"Beginner's luck." "Very contagious beginner's luck."*

**High striker.** A RING THE BELL! tower with rungs from WET NOODLE up to CHAMPION!, and beside it a big sign, GRAND PRIZE: FROZEN TURKEY, over a turkey on a tray of ice. The professor chalks up F = m·a and a lever (*"...and a lever does the rest. Observe."*), swings, and the puck creeps up to WET NOODLE and slides back down. *"In theory, the bell rang."* *"It says wet noodle, Professor."* The doctor demonstrates safe lifting, *"always with the knees"*, strains, gets the hammer over his head, and it tips him over backward. THUD! From the ground, a thumbs up: *"Doctor's orders: bed rest."* *"Clearly, the bell is miscalibrated."*

**The kids.** *"Can we try?" "All together now!"* The kids set their prizes on a crate, and all four lift one hammer, wind up, jump and slam it down. WHAM!! The puck rockets up the tower, DING! The bell rings, the lights chase, confetti bursts: WINNER!

**The frozen turkey.** The kids lift the prize off the ice: a huge, frost-covered, shrink-wrapped turkey in a white net bag, with an orange FROZEN TURKEY · 20 LB label and a GRAND PRIZE rosette. *"C-c-cold!" "It's frozen solid!"* It goes round the kids like a hot potato until Alma throws it to the professor (*"Professor, catch!"*), who juggles it into the doctor's arms, where it sticks. *"My hands are stuck!" "Fascinating. Basic thermodynamics."*

**The promise.** Holding it up together, the brothers make a promise. The professor will cook it for Thanksgiving; he is the best turkey cook there is, with recipes rooted in science (a board: TURKEY SCIENCE, Q = m·c·ΔT). The doctor will make the best healthy stuffing (a chart: kale, quinoa, broccoli). The kids trade looks. *"...Uh oh." "Have they ever cooked a turkey?" "Statistically improbable." "I'll bring pizza."* The brothers hoist the turkey: *"You shall see!"* Freeze. TO BE CONTINUED… The iris closes on the frosty turkey.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Timing:** `T` in `scene.js` holds the start of every line and action beat. The lines themselves (text, speed, hold) are in `asset.js`; if you move a line, move its entry in `T` too. Shots cut on `T` values, and every line ends before the next cut.
- **Borrowed set:** `asset.js` `uses: ['scene71_festival_games']`, so scene 71's `props.js` (`FF`) loads first: booths, banner, hay, leaves, `FF.prize`, `FF.ring`, `FF.card`. Its prize racks are drawn empty (`gone`), since the kids won them in part 1.
- **Blocking:** both games are lanes along X at `FF2.LANE_Z` (1780, in front of scene 71's games). The bean bag mark is `FF2.BX` (in front of the BEAN BAG TOSS booth), the high striker mark is `FF2.HX` (in front of the ring toss booth). The group stands left of the mark at the `slot` offsets in `CAST` (as in scene 71); `MARKS` says who is on the mark and when. For the kids' swing they stand at `SWINGPOS` (around the hammer), and for the turkey handover at `TABLEPOS` (around the prize table, `FF2.DX`). They move by cuts only.
- **Bean bags:** `B0` (the professor: over the board into the pumpkin stack), `B1` (the doctor: into the popcorn at `FF2.POPX`), `HK` (Housam's drop, taps and volley; `TAPP` are the tap points) and `AK` (Alma's toss). `bagAt(t)` gives every bag in flight. `ringAt(t)` is the part-1 ring flying off and rolling away.
- **High striker:** `hammerAt(t)` gives the hammer as a grip centre `G` and a direction `phi` (keyframes `REST`, `UP`, `HIT`, `UPK`, `KHIT`, `DROP`). The swinger's hands are IK'd onto the handle (`toLocal`), and the kids each grip the handle point nearest their shoulder. A raised hammer is drawn behind the swinger. The doctor's lift and fall is `drawSamiLift` (the body rotates about his feet). `puckY(t)` drives the puck; `SAFHIT`, `KIDHIT`, `DING` are the hits.
- **The turkey:** `FF2.turkey(x, y, s, t, o)` (base middle, px per world unit) is the shared design: ice-blue and frosty, shrink-wrapped, a white net bag gathered with a clip, an orange FROZEN TURKEY · 20 LB label, a red-and-gold GRAND PRIZE rosette, frost crystals and cold mist. In this scene it is drawn at `TS` = 1.35 world scale. `turkeyAt(t)` gives its world position and holders (the lift, `HOPS`, the throw, the catch, the toss, the shared hold, the hoist); holders' hands are placed at its sides (`TW`, `TH`).
- **Prizes:** the kids hold their part-1 prizes (`holding(t)`), except from `T.KIDS` to `T.PROMISE`, when they sit on a crate by the mark (`st.pile`).
- **Boards:** `safadiBoard` / `samiBoard` with `kind: 'none'`, and the chalk drawn on top by `chalkOn` (same pop and wobble): HIGH STRIKER PHYSICS, TURKEY SCIENCE, HEALTHY STUFFING.
- **Props** (`props.js`, namespace `FF2`): `FF2.dress(P, t, st)` returns the new games as depth-sorted items (the cornhole board, the pumpkin stack, the tower with its rungs, bulbs and bell, the prize table and its sign, the crate of prizes). Also `FF2.bag`, `FF2.mallet`, `FF2.popcorn`, `FF2.confetti`, `FF2.rain`, `FF2.title`.
