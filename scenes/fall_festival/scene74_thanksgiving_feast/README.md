## Synopsis

The fourth scene of the **Fall Festival** series. That morning (scene 73) the brothers burnt the festival turkey to charcoal, painted it with gravy, hid the worst under parsley and stuck a drumstick back on with a band-aid. Now it is evening and the table for twelve is set with candles. Everyone is hungry: *"I'm so hungry!"* *"I can eat anything!"* *"I was promised... a feast."*

**The grand entrance.** The brothers walk in through the archway with the platter under a cloche. The professor explains that the turkey was *"roasted by the laws of thermodynamics"*, and a chalk formula floats above him: *4 h × 350° = 1 h × 1400°*. The doctor adds his *"healthy stuffing: kale, quinoa, broccoli. Zero fat!"* The guests give a drumroll on the table, the cloche lifts (TA-DA!) and the glazed bird gleams. *"OOOOOH!"* Danny squints: *"Is that a... band-aid?"* *"Garnish."*

**The carving.** *"Allow me."* The knife hits the bird with a CLANK and bends, and the band-aided drumstick drops onto the platter. *"Self-carving. Very advanced."* The professor saws until the glaze flakes off, then the bird CRACKS open: a black crust, an icy blue centre breathing cold mist, and bright green stuffing. *"Why is it... blue?"* *"Who's hungry?!"* The doctor slides a plate along the table to every guest. *"Bon appétit!"*

**The tasting.** Ten bites, ten reactions:
- Fester gets the drumstick. CRUNCH, a tooth DING, swirly eyes: *"...and this is the story of my life."*
- Housam's fork bends. His slice bounces off his head (BONK) and lands back on the plate: *"Coefficient of restitution: point nine."*
- Alma tucks her slice under her napkin: *"...Delicious!"*
- Jenna offers a bite to her giraffe, which shakes its head: *"Giraffe says: no, thank you."*
- Danny taps his slice. TINK, TINK: *"It's a popsicle."*
- King Neptune: *"A turkey-sicle! Ha!"* He bites, then washes it down with a rainbow water blast from his trident (GULP! GULP!): *"Water you feeding us?!"*
- Dragon flame-roasts his slice (FWOOSH), chomps it and turns an even greener green: *"Nope. Even I can't."*
- Dr. G lifts the doctor's wristwatch out of her stuffing: *"Rule number one: no accessories in the food. ...But I love the kale."*
- Roudaynah takes a brave bite and smiles with love: *"It is... very... original."*
- Princess Pearl takes one regal nibble and a slow blink: *"Somebody... find me... ANOTHER COOK."*

**Talking around it.** The brothers keep smiling. *"The char! A Maillard reaction."* *"The frozen centre preserves the vitamins!"* *"My watch! A great source of iron."* *"Plus, it tells you when it's done!"* The watch alarm rings: BRRRING! One by one, ten untouched plates slide back to the middle of the table and pile up in a wobbly stack. *"...Hm. More for us."* The brothers bite. CRUNCH. They freeze mid-chew with frost on their chins.

**Dessert.** Roudaynah and Dr. G lift the pumpkin pies they brought: *"How about... dessert?"* Everyone shouts *"DESSERT!"*, autumn confetti falls, the pies land with a PLOP and slices with whipped cream slide out to every plate. *"Now this is a feast."* *"That's fin-tastic!"* Fester raises his fork: *"Three cheers for the pie chefs: Roudaynah and Dr. G!"*

**The promise.** The brothers stand, a little humbled but not beaten. *"We promise: we will make it up to you."* *"Next time... the PERFECT turkey!"* *"You shall see!"* The table laughs. The twins glance at each other and share a thought bubble: two tall chef's hats and a neon **TURKEY TAKEOVER**, which sets up the celebrity-chef music video in scene 75. Freeze. TO BE CONTINUED… Iris out.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Timing:** `T` in `scene.js` holds the start of every line and action beat. The lines themselves (text, speed, hold) are in `asset.js`; if you move a line, move its entry in `T` too. No two bubbles overlap (checked against each line's `.25 + chars / cps + hold`).
- **Cast and blocking:** `CAST` gives each character's rig, unit, height (for `K.seatY`), seat (or the host's X), head half-width (for bubbles) and its rig family `fam`. `FACE` and `F(n, kind)` translate expressions (`happy`, `gasp`, `wince`, `deadpan`, `laugh`, `worried`, `polite`) into each rig's own eyes, mouth and brows. `TALK` holds each family's lip-flap shapes.
- **Draw order** (`stage`): `back(splitZ 150)` blurred → the hosts and far-side guests, far to near, inside `K.seated` → `front(splitZ 150, toZ 190)` (only the tall end chairs, which are nearer than Z 150) → Neptune and Dragon → the pies held in hands → `front(splitZ 190)` (the table, and everything on it through `onTable`) → `overTable` (cutlery in hands, the knife, flying slices, the flame, the water blast, frost, the formula, the thought bubble, SFX).
- **Hands on the table:** seated rigs are clipped at the table's far edge, so a hand on the plate would vanish. Hands stop just above the far edge (`g.edgeS`), and the fork (drawn after the table) reaches down to the plate. `L(x, y)` turns a screen point into the rig's local units (Jenna's include her internal `JENNA_SCALE`).
- **The turkey:** `turkeyState(t)` gives TKY `'fixed'` (wear 0 → .12 at the CLANK → .92 by the CRACK; `drumstickOff` from T.DROP), then `'carved'`. The carved bird is drawn **mirrored**, so its carved-away stump is on the side where the bandaged drumstick fell off. The fallen drumstick (`TF4.drumstick`) lies on the platter until it is served to Fester. A spoonful of stuffing covers the carved bird's watch once Dr. G's plate goes out. `clocheState` lifts the cloche at T.LIFT and lowers it again at T.QDESS. `platterY` lowers the platter onto the table at T.SET.
- **Plates:** `plateAt(n, t)` slides each served plate out (`SERVE_ORDER`, .32 s apart) and back into the `STACK` (`BACK_ORDER`, .5 s apart; each plate hops onto the stack). `piePlateAt` sends a pie plate from each pie to every guest after T.PSERVE, with `eaten` rising. `servedPlate` handles the per-guest state of each plate (Fester's drumstick, Alma's napkin lump, Housam's airborne slice, Dragon's burnt slice, bites taken).
- **Cameras:** `WIDE`, `MED`, `CLOSE`, `HEAD`, `FOOT`, `HOSTCAM`, `CARVE`, `TURKEY`, `ARCH` and `ENDCAM`, all drifting and pushing slowly. `aim(n, { dist, dx, sy })` is a medium close-up of one guest with the head at screen y `sy`. Keep dining cameras at Z ≤ 840.
- **Props** (`props.js`, namespace `TF4`): knife and carving fork, dinner fork (bend, bites), the band-aided drumstick, a loose slice (burn), the watch (ring), Dragon's flame, cold mist, the napkin lump, pie plates, the plate stack, the chalk formula, chef's hats and the TURKEY TAKEOVER thought, queasy lines, frost, hearts, confetti, the title card and TO BE CONTINUED.
- **Voices:** all twelve characters have a `voice` in their `asset.js`. Group shouts use one `group` bubble, plus `silentBubble` entries so that several voices sound at once.
