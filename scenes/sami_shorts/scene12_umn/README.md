## Synopsis

Sami welcomes Alma and Housam and agrees to bring Jenna and Danny to the show. When he gets the show started at 15 seconds, Jenna, Alma, Danny, and Housam are already seated on small audience chairs. They stay seated for the rest of the scene, including the games, handshake, laughter, and confiscation. Roudaynah waves goodbye to Jenna and Danny. Jenna smiles at Alma and asks, “Should we get Derp and Gummy Blob married?”, then makes a funny face. Danny shows Housam his Nintendo Switch and says, “Let’s play Mario Kart”. Sami and Professor Safadi jump in to remind the kids to balance games with activities, take breaks, and put screens away at bedtime. Sarah frowns because she cannot come. Danny sets the Switch on a low stand, then shares a secret handshake with Housam. Roudaynah and Sarah walk away together while the cousins stay with Sami. Danny picks the Switch back up and plays with Housam while Jenna and Alma play with Derp and Gummy Blob. Dr. Sami becomes furious and yells, “I told you not to play on your Nintendo Switch!” The girls laugh, then return to their stuffies. Sami takes the console from the boys and holds it out of reach. Housam leans over and whispers a secret to Danny. Danny gives him a thumbs-up, and Housam chuckles. Danny raises his hand. Sami says, “Yes, Danny?” Danny asks, “Can I go to the bathroom?” Sami gives him a long, suspicious stare before replying, “Fine.” Housam laughs quietly again. The kids remain seated, and Sami keeps the Switch. Meanwhile, Roudaynah drives away with Sarah in the front passenger seat. Sarah screams angrily because she could not attend the show. Roudaynah keeps her hands on the steering wheel and responds, “Yah Allah Minek.”
