## Synopsis

The first scene of the **Fall Festival** series. The drop-off road in front of DCES is closed for the festival: a FALL FESTIVAL banner hangs between the canopy piers, with striped booths on the sidewalk, bunting, hay bales, pumpkins, a scarecrow, orange trees and falling leaves. The kids want the prizes, and Danny wants the glow-in-the-dark sword. The brothers are confident: *"Winning is simply a matter of science."* *"And steady hands."*

**Ring toss.** The professor's first ring clanks off a peg (*"a practice throw"*) and his second rings a pumpkin. The doctor's ring slips out backward and lands on the professor's head, where it stays. The professor explains that a ring is a torus in projectile motion with a two-centimeter margin of error, so *"statistically, nobody can win this game."* The doctor adds that throwing strains the rotator cuff and prescribes rest for both of them. Alma then rings all three pegs and Jenna stacks a fourth. Alma wins the glow-in-the-dark sword and Jenna a plush giraffe. *"…Statistically improbable."*

**Archery.** The doctor pops a balloon, then shoots the scarecrow's hat, and changes the subject to posture and eight glasses of water a day. The professor takes the bow to demonstrate elastic potential energy, and his arrow drops at his feet. Housam hits the bullseye, and Danny, eyes closed, splits Housam's arrow. They win a plush dragon and a robot.

The brothers save face (*"In theory, I won."* *"In my medical opinion… nobody saw that."*). Alma points her sword at the bean bag toss, and the brothers vow: *"This time… we win!"* Freeze. The iris closes on the sword, which keeps glowing in the dark.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Timing:** `T` in `scene.js` holds the start of every line and action beat. The lines themselves (text, speed, hold) are in `asset.js`; if you move a line, move its entry in `T` too.
- **Blocking:** each game is a lane along X at `FF.LANE_Z` (on the drop-off road). The player stands on the mark (`FF.RINGX` for ring toss, `FF.ARCHX` for archery) and the target is about 400 units to the right. Everyone else stands in a group to the left of the mark, at the `slot` offsets in `CAST`. `MARKS` says who is on the mark and when; shots cut whenever that changes, so nobody has to walk.
- **Ring toss:** `THROWS` lists every ring (who, when, and where it ends: `bounce`, `pumpkin`, `head` or `peg`). `ringAt` gives a ring's position in flight; `gameState` tells the props which rings sit on which peg.
- **Archery:** `SHOTS` lists every arrow (`balloon`, `hat`, `plop`, `bull`, `split`). `arrowAt` gives the arrow in flight; `gameState` passes the popped balloon, the arrow in the hat and the arrows in the target to the props.
- **Poses:** `pose(n, t, a)` is one function for all six, organised by time. `throwPose` and `archerPose` are shared; `CAST` holds each character's hand heights (`rest`, `sh` shoulder, `up`, `side`) in local units.
- **Prizes:** Alma's sword, Housam's dragon and Danny's robot are drawn by `FF.prize` at their hands (`_sword`, `_dragon`, `_robot` in the pose). Jenna's giraffe is her rig's own plush (`giraffe: true` after `T.PRIZE1`). A prize leaves its booth rack when it is won.
- **Cameras** are offsets from the current lane's mark, so the same `GROUP`, `PROF` and `LANE` framings work for both games.
- **Props** (`props.js`, namespace `FF`): `FF.dress(P, t, state)` returns the festival as depth-sorted items; `FF.grade()` warms the school for autumn; `FF.leaves(t)` are the falling leaves. The BEAN BAG TOSS booth in the middle is there for the next scene.
